Who I Am

Driven by hard problems,
grounded in rigorous science.

I specialize in solving real-world challenges through advanced machine learning — spanning two domains I am deeply passionate about: Search & Recommendation Systems and Biomedical AI.

Currently, I architect AI discovery systems at IEEE that help engineers and researchers navigate 200M+ publications through natural language. Before that, I spent two years at Harvard, Dana-Farber, and the Broad Institute pushing the frontier of multi-modal genomics.

My Ph.D. at Boston University — recognized with the 2023 Outstanding Dissertation Award — focused on distributionally robust learning-to-rank frameworks for document retrieval and drug recommendation, now deployed across biomedical research pipelines.

I am constantly seeking new avenues to apply expertise and create impact at the intersection of AI, engineering, and human health.
